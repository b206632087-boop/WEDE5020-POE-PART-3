About Tumi's Bakery

Our Story

Tumi's Bakery was founded in 2022 right here in Pretoria with one goal: to bring people together with fresh, homemade baked goods made with love. What began in a home kitchen has now grown into a beloved local favourite for custom cakes and daily bread.

Our Mission

We believe every celebration deserves a sweet centerpiece. We use only quality ingredients and bake everything fresh daily to make sure your cakes, breads and pastries taste as good as they look.

Why Choose Us

  • Fresh daily: Nothing is baked the day before
  • Custom Orders: We bring your cakes to life
  • Local:Proudly Serving the Pretoria community
  • Made With Love: Famliy recipes passed down
